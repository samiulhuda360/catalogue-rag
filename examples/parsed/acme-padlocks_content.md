## Acme P30 padlock

* 30mm stainless body, 6mm shackle
* Shackle clearance 30mm
* Keyed alike or keyed to differ
* Supplied with 4 keys

---

## Acme P40 padlock

* 40mm hardened steel body, 8mm shackle
* Shackle clearance 60mm
* Keyed alike or keyed to differ
* Supplied with 2 keys

---

## Acme P50 padlock

* 50mm brass body, 10mm shackle
* Shackle clearance 60mm
* Key retaining: the key cannot be removed while open
* Supplied with 4 keys

---

## Acme P60 padlock

* 60mm brass body, 11mm shackle
* Shackle clearance 25mm
* Keyed alike or keyed to differ
* Supplied with 3 keys

---

## Acme P70 padlock

* 70mm brass body, 13mm shackle
* Shackle clearance 25mm
* Key retaining: the key cannot be removed while open
* Supplied with 2 keys
