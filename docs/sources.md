# Data sources

All catalogue knowledge used by this project comes from publicly available sources: product catalogues and brochures that manufacturers publish for free download on their own websites. No internal, confidential or paid material was used.

## Evaluation corpus

The results in [`eval/results/REPORT.md`](../eval/results/REPORT.md) were measured on 54 publicly available door
hardware catalogues and brochures (locks, closers, access control, padlocks, sliding and window hardware, safes),
downloaded from manufacturers' public websites in April 2026: 3,062 passages after chunking.

- The documents belong to their publishers. They are **not included** in this repository, and neither is any
  text extracted from them.
- The evaluation questions and saved answers quote those documents, so they are not published either; the
  method, metrics and summary results are.
- This is an independent portfolio project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or built for any manufacturer.

## Bundled sample

[`examples/parsed/`](../examples/parsed/) holds ten **fictional** "Acme" catalogues (56 pages) generated by
[`examples/make_sample.py`](../examples/make_sample.py), with matching evaluation questions in
[`examples/questions.jsonl`](../examples/questions.jsonl). Every product, part number and figure in them is
invented. The screenshots and the demo use this sample.

## Using your own documents

Put PDFs in `data/documents/`, then `python -m catalogue_rag parse` and `python -m catalogue_rag index`.
The system uses whatever is in `data/parsed_documents/`, and falls back to the sample when that folder is empty.
